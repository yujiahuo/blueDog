The service

Struggling with your dog's behavior? We can assess your dog through a holistic, expert lens and work with you to create an effective training plan that meets your goals. These appointments can cover anything from nuisance behaviors (such as chewing, barking, digging), to more serious issues (such as resource guarding, dog-dog aggression, dog-human aggression, separation anxiety, fear, or handling issues). Sarah is a Certified Dog Behavior Consultant who specializes in the tough cases and can make strong, lasting change without the use of aversive tools.

Sarah will take a complete history of your dog and the presenting problem, help you set realistic expectations for improvement, and present you with a detailed written plan tailored to work for your individual personality and lifestyle to improve your dog's behavior.

Follow-up via phone and email is encouraged and AT NO CHARGE!
