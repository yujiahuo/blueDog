Introducing the area's first Puppy Stay and Train

Need a few weeks of intensive, focused training, but don't have the time?

Stay and train may be the perfect option for you. And ours has a big focus on puppies! The first few weeks of a dog's life are when they're soaking up the world like sponges, and that time is absolutely critical. The choices we make can impact a dog's behavior for the rest of their life. With an expert eye, Sarah can spot "pink flags" early and prevent problems before they arise or bad habits start to form. Many issues later in life can be attributed to early experience and learning, and it's much harder to unlearn something than to learn it right from the start.

Sarah has raised hundreds of puppies into stellar adults and would love to give your puppy the same early boost toward success. Your puppy will be socialized extensively will all sorts of things in the world, get to interact with other dogs under expert supervision, and will work on potty training, crate training, grooming prep, foundational skills, and more. Your pup will get daily one-on-one attention from Sarah and come home with a fresh set of skills and manners. At the end of the program, you'll go home with a Skills Transfer appointment and video to review everything your dog learned here and give you the skills you need to maintain and build on what your dog learned.

We accept adolescent and adult dogs too

While puppies are our main focus, we can help a dog at any age.

Our program is great for:

  • Polite greeting
  • Leash walking
  • Continued socialization and confidence-building
  • Grooming and vet prep
  • Exposure and interactions with other dogs
  • Handling and grooming prep
  • Crate training
  • ...and more
Stay and train is not for dogs showing aggression or other more serious behavior problems. For those, see behavior consults.

Why choose us?

Trusting someone with your best friend isn't easy. Unlike other boarding programs, your dog will live in a home environment, not a kennel or a facility. We guarantee there will be no shocking, choking, or rough handling, and we will never ask you to use any methods you're not comforable with. Real, lasting results can absolutely be achieved without the use of physical punishment. Not only do we teach skills, but we teach the dog what to do and what no to do in the environment by default and make good choices on their own.

Pet sitting is available for dogs who have done a stay and train

Clients who have been through the program can also sign up for regular pet sitting or get a brush-up on skills while they're here. This is a helpful option if you want your dog to be boarded somewhere familiar with someone they know who can be consistent with their training.
