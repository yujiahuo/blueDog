Why choose positive reinforcement training with Blue Dog?

The dog training industry is unregulated

There are no strict requirements that someone has to meet before they can call themselves a trainer or behaviorist and work with your dog, so it's important to look for certifications from reputable organizations. To us, getting properly certified anyway means committing to modern, evidence based practice. We maintain these certifications through continuing education, making sure we keep up with the industry as it evolves. You can be confident that we won't be using your grandma's training plan.

We are committed to humane, modern training techniques

No dog needs a heavy hand, regardless of breed, size, or temperament. We've spent decades working successfully with the toughest cases, including issues of aggression, reactivity, and much more. We train without pain, fear, or intimidation and do not use aversive tools such as prong collars or e-collars. Dogs are sentient, emotional animals, and they are members of our family. We owe it to them to teach and lead with kindness.

"The use of shock in training and behavior is not considered a best practice by the IAABC or the Joint Standards of Practice, and is strongly discouraged."

- IAABC position statement

If you were to ask me why I choose a positive reinforcement approach with dog training…

I'd tell you how much I think about the animal experience in the human world and how hard it must be to navigate an existence where you don't speak the language and you're expected to follow arbitrary rules that go against the behavior you were literally designed to do. I'd tell you how nothing makes me happier than my dog and cats being 100% themselves because they're not afraid of any consequences. I'd tell you how I think it's my job as their mom to help them be successful and make sure they are living their best life, and how for me, that involves compassion and understanding, not compliance. And I'd say that at the end of the day, I jus don't want to be a source of any worry, fear, discomfort, or pain for my pets, so I'm so glad that positive reinforcement has given me so many ways to navigate our life together with trust & joy.

- Juliana DeWillems
