A la carte skills training

Already got the foundations down? Sometimes we just need a brush-up, or to level up specific skills. Skills Training sessions are for dogs who already have some training and want to focus their time on a particular skill with the guidance of someone who really knows their stuff.

*Only for existing Blue Dog clients, or new clients with a Rock-solid Fundamentals + Skills package.

Common choices for skills to work on include:

  • Leash walking
  • Recall
  • Nuisance behaviors like jumping up
  • ...and more

Seasonal offering - Out and About

In the warm weather months, we offer this special version of skills training. You and Sarah will take your dog out around town and work on skills together. Perfect for working on recall in the park, attention in public, and more.
