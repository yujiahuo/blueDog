Speaking around the world

Certified Dog Behavior Consultant / Certified Pet Dog Trainer, Sarah Kalnajs, is an internationally renowned, engaging and popular speaker on topics relating to dogs and their behavior. Sarah's presentations range from those designed to educate children to advanced topics for dog professionals. Presentations include a wealth of live video footage and support materials tailored to the topic and the audience.

With an extensive background in the area of canine behavioral signals / body language and their meanings, Sarah's presentations are invaluable for a wide range of audiences, including:

  • Veterinary Clinic Staff / Veterinary Students / Vet Tech Students
  • Dog Trainers and Behavior Consultants
  • Dog Daycare Staff / Groomers / Breeders
  • Animal Shelters and Rescue Groups
  • Utility and Public Works Crews / Police Departments
  • Pet Owners

As a Certified Dog Behavior Consultant / Certified Pet Dog Trainer, Sarah is uniquely qualified to speak on any dog behavior / training topic of interest to your organization and offers a wide range of topics.

Seminar speaker

Sarah has spoken at Clicker Expo, the Aggression in Dogs Conference, and some other international conferences. She's been a guest on several podcasts.

Shelter staff training

Sarah has worked with shelters and alongside rescues for 20 years and does periodic talks for a local rescue. She can help train staff on how to recognize body language, how to introduce themselves to dogs who may be more nervous, how to handle tricky dogs, and much more.

Professional development talks

Do your staff often encounter animals when they visit clients? Sarah shares her tips on how to communicate with owners, stay safe, and make sure everyone is comfortable while work gets done.
