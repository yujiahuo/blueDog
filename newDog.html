The service

Rock-solid fundamentals are for anyone who wants to make sure they're doing all the right things from the beginning to make sure their dog develops into a wonderful, behaviorally sound companion. Whether you've trained a dog before and want to streamline the usual stuff, or if you're brand new to the world of dogs and want to know all the most important bits, fundamentals are for you. We'll cover information that will make the biggest difference to you and your dog and learn about common mistakes new dog owners make so you don't fall into those traps.

Fundamentals are also tailored to your individual dog and their genetics. We'll discuss breed and individual traits, and how we can respect and appreciate them while also teaching the dog how to be a good citizen.

This is especially great for:

  • New puppies whose early experiences can make all the difference in who they grow into
  • New dogs joining a family and adapting to their new life
  • Anyone looking to start building a relationship with their dog. It's never too late to start!

Fundamentals are not for dogs who already have significant behavior problems.
