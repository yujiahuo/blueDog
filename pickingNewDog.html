Looking for your next dog?

Want to make sure the dog you're considering is safe and a good choice for your lifestyle and environment?

Having conducted almost a thousand temperament tests of her own research and design, Sarah Kalnajs can help with this important choice. A Blue Dog temperament test is similar to a personality test in people. It can tell you what the dog will act like in many situations to a high probability. It can tell you if the dog has a tendency towards aggression or resource guarding. It can tell you whether the dog is a good choice for a home with children or a home that is planning to have children during the dog's lifetime.

With so many dogs adopted and then surrendered because they weren't a good "fit", many clients have been thrilled with the help from Blue Dog in selecting their new pet. Let us help you avoid future heartbreaking mistakes.

Options at any stage of the process

I'm at the very beginning of my dog-selecting journey

We'll talk about all the things! We'll talk about different breeds, ages, activity levels, and more. We'll dive in at a level of detail you won't often find from a simple Google search. Many traits are frequently glossed over or explained in a way that doesn't give you a clear picture of what your life will be like once you actually live with it. We'll discuss all the places you can get a dog, and the pros and cons of different choices.

I've got a kind of dog in mind, but need help choosing an individual

No two puppies are alike, even from the same litter. If you've already got a litter in mind, whether from a breeder or a rescue, we can drive out with you and help make the selection. Or if there isn't a litter, we can help find the right litter as part of a customizable package.

I've got a specific dog in mind

Whether this is a puppy or an adult, we can do a temperament test and answer all your questions to see if the dog would be a good fit. In order to get a temperament test, Sarah will either need to be able to meet the dog in person, or you'll need to be able to provide like a bunch of videos probably.
