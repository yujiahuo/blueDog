The service

There can be many advantages to training your own service dog. You'll have a change to build your relationship from the start, and it's often much more economical. We can guide you on this journey through task training, public access, and more to make sure your service dog has a long and successful career ahead of them.

These sessions are perfect for:

  • Dogs and puppies in the very beginning of their journey
  • Service dogs in training who want to refine some of their skills
  • ESAs who want to refine their skills
